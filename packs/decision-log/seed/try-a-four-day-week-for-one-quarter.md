## Context

Two people have said they are close to burning out; output has been flat for
two quarters. A trial, not a policy: Fridays off for one quarter, same pay,
same goals.

**State of mind:** afternoon, slightly anxious about how it looks to
customers.

## Options

### A — Four-day week, everyone, one quarter

- Good: clear signal; easy to measure against last quarter.
- Bad: support coverage on Fridays; a customer may notice.

### B — Flexible Fridays, optional

- Good: no coverage gap.
- Bad: the people who most need it will not take it.

### Do nothing

- Good: no risk this quarter.
- Bad: the risk is already here.

## Decision

Leaning A. Not decided until the support rota question is answered.

## Expected outcome

Output at least flat, sick days down, nobody resigns. Confidence 55% — a
coin toss with a good downside, since it reverses in a day. What would prove
this wrong: a missed customer deadline traced to the Friday gap.

## Consequences

Easier: hiring, morale. Harder: Friday coverage. Given up: about ten percent
of calendar time, which we do not believe is ten percent of output.

## Review

Not yet decided, so no `review` date. If accepted, set one for the end of the
quarter and judge it against the same three numbers named above.
