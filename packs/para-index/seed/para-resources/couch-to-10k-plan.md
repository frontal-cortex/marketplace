## Why I kept this

The training plan for the 10k: three runs a week for twelve weeks, with the
long run growing by a kilometre each week.

## Key points

- Never increase weekly distance by more than 10%.
- Week 6 and week 10 are easy weeks on purpose.

## Highlights

> Consistency beats intensity. Three short runs beat one heroic one.
