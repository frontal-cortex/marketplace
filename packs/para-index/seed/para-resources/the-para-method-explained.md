## Why I kept this

The original description of the method these databases follow, for when you
wonder whether something is a project or an area.

## Key points

- Sort by actionability, not by topic: Projects, then Areas, then Resources, then Archive.
- A project has an outcome and an end; an area has a standard and no end.
- Move things between buckets freely as their actionability changes; archive, do not delete.

## Highlights

> Projects are what you are working on now. Areas are what you are responsible for. Resources are what you are interested in. Archives are what you have set aside.
