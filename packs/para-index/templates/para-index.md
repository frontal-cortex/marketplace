The **PARA method**: everything you keep falls into one of four buckets,
sorted by how actionable it is right now — not by topic.

## Projects
*Short-term efforts with a deadline and a defined outcome.*

- [[Project name]] — one line on where it stands

## Areas
*Ongoing responsibilities with no end date: health, finances, a role at work.*

- [[Area name]]

## Resources
*Reference material you might need later — no active commitment attached.*

- [[Resource name]]

## Archive
*Anything from the above that is inactive. Move it here; don't delete it.*

- [[Archived item]]

---
**Review cadence:** revisit weekly (the Weekly Review template has a slot
for it). Move finished Projects to Archive; promote a Resource to a Project
when it becomes actionable.
