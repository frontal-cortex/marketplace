## Why I picked this up

Kept seeing it cited as the book that made "what if" fiction about people
rather than machines.

## Key ideas

- Trust between two people is built in the slow, boring middle of a journey,
  not in the crisis.
- A society's assumptions are invisible to it until someone from outside
  bumps into them.

## Quotes worth keeping

> Light is the left hand of darkness, and darkness the right hand of light.

## Verdict

Five out of five; reread in winter. Lend it to anyone who says they do not
read science fiction.
