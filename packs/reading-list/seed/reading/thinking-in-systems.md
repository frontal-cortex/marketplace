## Why I picked this up

Wanted a plain-language grounding in stocks, flows and feedback before
redesigning how the team reports progress.

## Key ideas

- A system's behaviour comes from its structure, not from the individual
  events you notice.
- Delays in a feedback loop cause oscillation; shortening the delay is often
  worth more than strengthening the response.

## Quotes worth keeping

> The behavior of a system cannot be known just by knowing the elements of
> which the system is made.
