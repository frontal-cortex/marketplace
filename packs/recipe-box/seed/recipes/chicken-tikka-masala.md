## Ingredients

- [ ] 600 g chicken thighs, cubed
- [ ] 150 g yoghurt
- [ ] 2 tbsp garam masala
- [ ] 1 tbsp ground cumin
- [ ] 1 tsp turmeric
- [ ] 1 onion, finely chopped
- [ ] 3 cloves garlic and a thumb of ginger, grated
- [ ] 1 tin (400 g) chopped tomatoes
- [ ] 150 ml double cream
- [ ] Coriander, rice to serve

## Method

1. Mix the chicken with the yoghurt, half the garam masala, the cumin and turmeric. Marinate an hour, or overnight.
2. Grill or sear the chicken hard until charred at the edges; set aside.
3. Soften the onion in oil, add garlic and ginger, then the rest of the garam masala.
4. Add the tomatoes and simmer ten minutes. Blend smooth if you like.
5. Stir in the cream and the chicken; simmer ten minutes more. Finish with coriander.
