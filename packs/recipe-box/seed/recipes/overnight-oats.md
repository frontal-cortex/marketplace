## Ingredients

- [ ] 50 g rolled oats
- [ ] 120 ml oat milk
- [ ] 1 tbsp chia seeds
- [ ] 1 tsp maple syrup
- [ ] A pinch of salt
- [ ] Fruit and nuts to top

## Method

1. Stir everything but the toppings together in a jar.
2. Lid on, fridge overnight.
3. Top with fruit and nuts in the morning; loosen with a splash of milk if it is too thick.

## Notes

Make four jars on Sunday. Grated apple and cinnamon is the best version.
