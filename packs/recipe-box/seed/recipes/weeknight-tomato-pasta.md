## Ingredients

- [ ] 400 g spaghetti
- [ ] 2 tins (800 g) chopped tomatoes
- [ ] 3 cloves garlic, sliced
- [ ] 4 tbsp olive oil
- [ ] 1 tsp chilli flakes
- [ ] A handful of basil
- [ ] Parmesan, salt, pepper

## Method

1. Put a large pan of salted water on to boil.
2. Warm the oil in a wide pan over a medium heat; add the garlic and chilli and cook until the garlic is pale gold, about two minutes.
3. Add the tomatoes and a pinch of salt. Simmer for fifteen minutes, crushing the tomatoes with a spoon, until thick.
4. Cook the spaghetti until just short of done. Lift it into the sauce with a cup of pasta water and toss over the heat for a minute.
5. Tear in the basil, grate over parmesan, season.

## Notes

Doubles well; the sauce freezes for three months. A tin of anchovies melted in
with the garlic makes it a different dish.
